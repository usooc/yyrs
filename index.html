FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Payments, escrow, fees, listing links, declining a trade, and privacy — tap a question to expand.

How do I pay? ▼

The platform uses USDT as the only payment method.

Network: BNB Chain (BSC / BEP20)

⚠️ Network, token, and amount must all be correct. Losses from any mistake cannot be recovered. The payer bears the loss; the platform and sellers are not responsible.

How does the platform handle rule-breaking users? ▼

Anyone attempting fraud, improper profit, or other abuse on this platform faces zero tolerance.

Once verified, we will:

  • Permanently ban the account and provide no further service
  • Automatically clear all related data
  • Leave any resulting loss to be borne by the user

The platform serves users with genuine needs. A healthy trading environment is maintained by the whole Yiyuan community.

I paid, but the seller says they didn’t receive the money ▼

To protect both sides, buyer payments are held in escrow on the platform.

Funds are released to the seller only after the seller actually ships, and the buyer confirms the goods are valid and received.

The amount I received from a sale looks wrong ▼

Each sale includes a platform service fee covering buyer/seller protection, on-chain fees, and operating costs.

Current fee: 5% (minimum 1.2 USDT).

How can I help buyers order my item faster? ▼
  1. Recommended: go to Seller hub → On sale, long-press the title (or right-click on desktop) to copy the link, then send it to the buyer.
  2. Go to Seller hub → On sale, tap the item number, then Copy link. The link is copied automatically; send it to the buyer.
  3. In the item’s site channel, forward the listing message or copy that message’s link. Check carefully so you don’t send someone else’s item.

💡 Method 1 has the fewest steps and is least likely to send the wrong item.

A buyer paid, but I don’t want to trade ▼

If you have concerns about the buyer’s reputation, stock, or other reasons, you may decline the trade.

After the payment notice, tap Decline trade. The buyer’s payment is refunded automatically, and no order record is created.

What trading methods does the platform support? ▼

The platform does not restrict how buyers and sellers trade, but we only protect trades completed inside the platform.

Private / off-platform trades are not covered, and they create no order record here.

Is my information safe on the platform? ▼

📌 If you have used the platform and have trade records, some information remains — especially phone numbers and shipping addresses.

There is no system entry to query or export user data. Even admins cannot view private user data in normal operations. Only during a dispute can relevant information be seen for handling.

🔒 We store it, but we cannot use it.
