HOW IT WORKS

How a trade is completed

From browsing to reviews, the steps are fixed and visible. Off-platform address or amount changes are not covered.

1

Browse / list

Search by site or across the market; sellers submit items for review.

2

Order & escrow

A one-time address is created; pay the shown BNB Chain USDT amount.

3

Ship & verify

Sellers ship on time and upload proof; physical orders get tracking checks.

4

Confirm receipt

Buyer confirmation pays the seller; overdue receipt follows platform rules.

5

Reviews / disputes

Both sides review; disputes need evidence within the deadline and are decided by the rules.
